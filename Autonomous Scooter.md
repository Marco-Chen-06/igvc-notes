## Components
- 1 Elegoo Mega 2560 R3 Board
- 2 MD20C R2 30A DC Motor Drivers
- 1 Tobsun 50W DC-DC converter (12-24V input to 5V 10A max output)
- LSM6DS3 IMU (Sparkfun Breakout Board)
- 2 motors probably

## Architecture
- Encoder interrupts
- Not sure, but definitely not differential drive
- ROS and Velodyne Lidar were used
