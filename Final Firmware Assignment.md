From here on, no more weekly assignments! You are responsible for your section, so here are the general expectations:

- Weekly: Populate the fields with your progress or goals/plans, questions, and confidence level for each week. This is so we can all see the progress in each other's subsection so we can plan on when to combine and integrate stuff. Also it looks cool
- Every meeting: Show your latest thing working/progress, even if it is small. If you had nothing done for the week, i completely understand, because cooper is a hard school. Momentum is pretty important, but grinding out a system in a week is also a solid strategy.
- Early December: The goal is that every section is able to control their relevant firmware using their own picos on a breadboard.
